# Label Taxonomy Notes

The fault-text classifier should keep labels operationally useful rather than overly fine-grained.

Recommended label groups:

- Electrical faults: power supply, motor, wiring, cabinet, and sensor circuit issues.
- Mechanical faults: bearing, belt, vibration, leakage, and abnormal wear.
- Process faults: temperature, pressure, flow, material blockage, and abnormal output.
- Safety or downtime risk: cases requiring escalation or immediate shutdown review.

Ambiguous repair tickets should keep the original wording in review notes so future relabeling is possible.
